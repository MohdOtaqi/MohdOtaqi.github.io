Laffa Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · App: Laffa (com.otaqi.laffa) · Developer: Mohammad Otaqi

What stays on your phone

Player names, your own truths and dares, settings, scores and game history are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, in order to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (Google's consent form), and you can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad & privacy choices. See how Google uses data. Buying the full version removes ads.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you own the full version; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. We never see your payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't sell personal data.

Children

Laffa is a general-audience party game, not directed at children under 13. Its content is family-friendly, and Family mode hides the packs that aren't meant for kids (Friends: Bold, Couples, Deep Talk).

Contact
